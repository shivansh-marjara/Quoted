# First user

A freelancer you already know. Twenty minutes. Do this on 18 October.

## Before

1. They do not need a CSV. Excel, a photo of a notebook, a PDF, Word, JSON, or typing three jobs from memory is enough.
2. If they do have a list, ask them to bring finished jobs: hours promised, hours it took, money charged.
3. If they refuse a file, sit with them and type from memory. Gut numbers written down still count.

## Session (20 minutes)

1. Open the live link on their laptop. No signup.
2. They land on their own empty notebook. Ask them to type three finished jobs, or import a file.
3. If they freeze, press **See an example notebook**, then **These are my jobs** and type theirs.
4. Paste one thing someone asked them to do this week.
5. Press **What should I promise this time?** Look at the number together.

Record 60–90 seconds: unfinished jobs in red, then the next promise filling.

## Three questions (write the answers verbatim)

1. Would you pay Rs 499 a month for this?
2. What did it get wrong?
3. What do you want it to tell you next?

Paste those three answers into the DEV post under **My First User**. If they did not pay, say so.

## If the friend is late (sample notebook)

1. Open Quoted. Click **Dentist website by Friday**.
2. Press **What should I promise this time?**
3. Point at **19 hours** and **₹28,500**. Say: you said 10, it took 19, so do not promise 10 again.
4. Click the coaching control panel. Show the hours change.
